## PawHaven UI scope

**The aesthetics above are overridden by this repo's design system.** "Choose distinctive,
characterful fonts, avoid Inter" · "gradient meshes, noise textures, geometric patterns" · "break
conventions: asymmetry, overlap, diagonal flow" · "custom cursors" — none of it applies here. Taste
is expressed by choosing well from the committed system, not by inventing values.

Load `style` before writing any styling and `component` before deciding where a component lives.
Both are already granted, and between them they own token selection, utility choice, and the
`@pawhaven/ui` / `@pawhaven/frontend-core` split.

There is no Figma, no design spec, and no screenshot to match. Do not ask for one.
